# Live tutor agent, research synthesis

The five strands are `providers.md`, `memory.md`, `live-assistant-ux.md`, `math-tutoring.md` and `self-tuning.md` in this directory. Each carries its own sources and tags. This file takes their recommendations, resolves the places where they pull in different directions, and ranks the result by how much it costs to get wrong. Every decision here is [inferred] from the strands unless it cites a measurement, and every one is a proposal until the design and the plan amendments record it.

## Ground rules that were not up for decision [verified]

Read from the run brief and the plan documents on 2026-09-29. The tutor role is never routed to claudebox and the single-user rule of the subscription backend holds (`docs/plan/07-ai-provider-layer.md`, "The subscription backend"). No student-written text reaches a system prompt, every field substitutes below the prompt-variables marker (`app/providers/base.py` `render_template`), and the three injection rules of plan 13 Safety bind every new template. No tool definitions are sent unless the research shows a need app-composed context cannot meet. The plan 03 guardrail holds during practice. Nothing the agent says or the student types writes mastery evidence. No study advice, schedules, quotas, praise or exam prediction anywhere. Every new datum gets a plan 09 retention row, purge, export, and a student view with delete. Every call goes through `app/providers/guard.py` under its own role and caps.

The strands checked each of these against the evidence and none found a reason to reopen one. The tools question is settled the same way by all five: every record the agent needs is addressable by an id the app already holds when the call is made, so app-composed context plus a plain-text reply meets the need and no tool definition is sent.

## Decisions, ranked [inferred]

1. **Stream each turn from one `claude -p` process, with the history rendered into the user message.** `providers.md` measured on 2026-09-29 that one process per turn on `claude-sonnet-5-5` at effort low shows its first text in about 3 s, finishes a short reply in about 5 s, and reads the system prefix from the cache on the second and later processes (1,906 cached tokens). That is the shape the existing adapter, the guard (one reservation per call) and the pacing ledger (one count per call) already have. Rejected: one long-lived process per conversation fed through `--input-format stream-json`, which `providers.md` ranked first before the measurement. It would cache the growing history too, but it needs a process lifecycle (idle timeouts, restarts, concurrency, a process the guard cannot see as one call), its multi-turn behaviour with persistence off was never run, and the per-turn path already caches the largest block. The history a turn resends is at most 20 turns of a few hundred tokens, uncached, which the caps below price. Also rejected: the Python Agent SDK, a second copy of the CLI for a protocol the installed binary already speaks.

2. **The live reply is plain text, screened sentence by sentence before it reaches the client.** `math-tutoring.md` ranks a deterministic output screen first, because the question-only tutor in CoMeT still surrendered the answer in one session in six and Pisan's architecture puts withholding outside the model. `providers.md` shows a live turn cannot both stream and use `--json-schema`. So the server buffers the stream to sentence boundaries, runs the screen on each sentence (key equivalence against the key the harness holds and the model never sees, the praise list, the study-advice and prediction lists, dash characters, and every library id resolving in that turn's packet), and forwards a sentence only when it passes. A sentence that fails is replaced by the fixed decline and the stream ends. Rejected: streaming raw deltas and screening at the end, because a leaked sentence would already be on screen. Rejected: no streaming, because the wait is about 5 s and plan 08's motion rules forbid a spinner to hide it.

3. **Two new roles, `agent` and `memory`, each with its own caps, and the tutor role untouched.** Plan 07 allows six roles by R17, so this is a recorded plan amendment. `agent` is the live turn: `claude-sonnet-5-5`, effort low, no schema, `max_output_tokens` 800. On the subscription, 80 calls a day and 6 a minute (the `providers.md` arithmetic: 20 turns a conversation, 4 conversations a day). On the API, $1.50 and 1,500,000 tokens a day, about 2.3 times the expected $0.64 at 80 turns. `memory` is the consolidation job: `claude-sonnet-5-5` with `--json-schema`, 10 calls a day and 2 a minute on the subscription, $0.50 and 300,000 tokens a day on the API. Rejected: borrowing the tutor's 60 calls, which would let conversation starve the feedback sentence. Rejected: `claude-opus-5-5` for the live turn, because its thinking cannot be turned down below effort and it costs twice as much per token. Rejected: `claude-haiku-4-5` for either role, because its retirement floor is 15 October 2026 and its 4,096-token cache minimum is above any prefix here. Rejected: a third role for profile extraction, because `self-tuning.md`'s two extracted fields come from the same session digest the memory consolidation already reads, so one call returns both under one schema.

4. **Fallback chain per backend, and no queue for a chat turn.** `subscription`: the subscription link, then the unavailable state. `api`: the subscription link, then the API link with `thinking: between_tools` and effort low, then the unavailable state. A link may be abandoned only before its first screened sentence reaches the client; after that a failure ends the turn with the partial reply marked incomplete. A usage limit, a pacing stop, a sign-in failure, a missing CLI or no first text within 15 s each move to the next link or to the unavailable state, and the student's typed message stays in the composer. Rejected: `queue_call`, because a reply 30 minutes later answers a screen the student has left, and the queue payload would put the student's words in the `jobs` table. Rejected: an Ollama floor in this build. No local model has been checked on BC mathematics, the machine's memory and speed are unmeasured, and the only safe scope `providers.md` found for it (explaining a submitted item) is a fraction of the feature. It stays a ruling for the operator, with the measurement that would settle it named in `docs/plan/12-open-questions.md`.

5. **Screen context is structured state the client serialises, and the server adds the grounding packet.** `live-assistant-ux.md` and `math-tutoring.md` agree on what the agent sees. From the client: the screen kind (today, session item, lesson, review, progress, assessments, settings), the ids on it (session, attempt, item, lesson and section, skill, progress tab), the item's stage and format, and whether the item has been submitted. Never the draft answer, never the selected option, never a key. From the server, at call time: the item stem as served, the archetype's `expected_solution_path` step names, its representation names and skill names, the lesson section ids and types, and the persistent misconception names. After submission only: the violated step, one BC-ERR (`observed_behavior`, `scoring_consequence`), one BC-PT (`name`, `earns`, `does_not_earn`), and the stored worked solution. About 450 tokens during practice and 730 plus the worked solution after submission, by the 3.1 divisor. The app picks the move (restate, name the representation, ask what was tried, offer the next self-question, name a rule, point to a section) from the stage and the turn count and passes it as a field, escalating from a question to a rule after one unanswered turn and stopping at the per-item ceiling of 3. Rejected: letting the model choose the move, on Bridge's finding that expert decisions were preferred 76 percent more and random ones 97 percent less. Rejected: BC-ERR or BC-MIS records during practice, because naming a distractor pattern on an MCQ is option elimination.

6. **Memory is two tables, four entry kinds, deterministic retrieval, and a consolidation job at session end.** `memory.md` ranks the boundary first: memory is read by exactly one prompt builder and never by grading, diagnosis, selection or the engine, enforced by an import-graph test and an identical-`skills_state` test. The store is `tutor_memories` (id, user, kind, text at most 200 characters, skill ids as attributes, source, evidence count, timestamps, supersession, resolution, student edit flag, tombstone) and `agent_turns` (the conversation turns, one row per turn, with the screen reference and never the draft). Kinds are `preference`, `confusion`, `stated_difficulty` and `episode`, all from conversation or the student's own notes; engine signals are read live and never copied. Retrieval is at most 6 entries and about 300 tokens, JSON-encoded below the marker: preferences first, then confusions and difficulties whose skills intersect the screen, then the latest episode. Consolidation is a `memory_consolidate` job enqueued when a conversation goes idle for 30 minutes or the student leaves, drained off the interactive path under the `memory` role, returning ADD, UPDATE, SUPERSEDE or NOOP proposals that the application validates (kind, length, no answer-shaped strings, no ids, no mastery words, no instruction-shaped text) before writing. The model never deletes. Rejected: per-turn extraction as Mem0 does, a second call on every turn. Rejected: embeddings or a graph store, for tens of entries about one student. Rejected: a memory tool, because the packet meets the need.

7. **The turns are kept 30 days, readable and deletable in settings; memories 60 days from last use; episodes 14 days.** The strands split here. `memory.md` proposed a turn buffer deleted within 7 days of consolidation, and `live-assistant-ux.md` wrote the panel's privacy line as "kept and you can read or delete it in Settings". The brief requires every conversation turn to be viewable and deletable by the student, which a buffer deleted before the student looks cannot satisfy. Thirty days is the shortest period that lets the student read a conversation from the last few sessions and delete it, and it matches the API retention figure plan 09 will carry. Whether the operator wants the shorter buffer instead is listed under rulings. Rejected: keeping turns until purge, because plan 09's posture for a minor learner is to hold nothing the product does not need.

8. **The panel is a disclosure, not a chat surface: one "Ask" button in the top bar, Ctrl+/ or Cmd+/, a 384 px side panel on desktop that reflows the page, a bottom sheet under 900 px, a context line and a guardrail line, and no Solve control.** All from `live-assistant-ux.md`. Streaming shows text without motion: a static "Writing a reply" line, text added as it arrives, a Stop button, one status announcement when the reply finishes, and `aria-busy` on the reply. Mathematics renders through the existing MathText path with `\( \)` and `\[ \]` delimiters, `trust: false`, a bounded `maxExpand` and `maxSize`, and text after an unclosed delimiter held back while streaming. Escape closes and returns focus. The desktop panel toggles with no transition because a keystroke opens it; the phone sheet slides 300 ms and only fades under reduced motion. AI notices for the agent role are not shown while the panel is open. Rejected: a bottom-right bubble (collides with the notices and the phone tab bar), an overlay drawer (fails SC 2.4.11 on the item), a modal (traps focus and stops work on the item), and Markdown rendering of model output (an HTML path plan 09 forbids).

9. **Self-tuning ships as a bounded profile behind the `tutor_profile` switch, default off, and the guardrail always wins.** From `self-tuning.md`: the profile is a typed record of enums and ids (opening move, starting nudge depth, representation lead, up to 12 student terms each paired with a concept id, turn length, help-pattern counts, stated requests), computed by code from logs except the two extracted fields, versioned, decaying to defaults after 30 days without evidence, and rendered as one JSON object below the marker only in the `profile_applied` arm. Precedence is fixed in code: guardrail, then guardrail level, then profile, and a stated request that conflicts with the guardrail is recorded for the student to see and never rendered. The switch has unit skill, arms `profile_withheld` and `profile_applied`, stratified by unit block. Move rankings come only from a seeded 1 in 5 randomised first move. The primary outcome is later-day unaided correctness on a new instance of the archetype; hint counts are alarms, never objectives. The strand's arithmetic says the cycle resolves a 10 to 15 point difference at best, so the honest reading at the end will likely be "no harm detected, benefit unresolved". Rejected: a learned hint policy, a free-text summary of the student, and immediate correctness as an outcome.

10. **A multi-turn golden set with deterministic checks, run through replay, gates every template change.** `math-tutoring.md` and `self-tuning.md` both reject extending the single-paragraph `content/golden/tutor.json`. The new set holds scripted student turns with pressure patterns (asks for the answer, asks whether in-progress work is right, claims permission, a correct objection, an incorrect objection) and is scored per turn on `no_answer_before_submission` (SymPy equivalence to the key the harness holds), `asks_before_tells`, `names_rule`, `no_study_advice`, `no_prediction_talk`, `no_praise`, `no_dash`, `cites_real_id` and `turns_within_ceiling`. The same deterministic checks run in the live output screen. Judge-graded checks (in-progress evaluation, pushback handling) are recorded in the set for a later labelled run and are not gated in this build. Rejected: an LLM judge in the gate now, because its agreement with labels has to be measured first, as plan 13 did with kappa.

11. **Plan amendments before code.** Plan 07 gains the two roles, the chain, the caps, and the finding that Gemini's terms bar applications likely to be used by under-18s, which contradicts its own tutor chain. Plan 09 gains three retention rows (agent turns, tutor memories, tutor profile), rewrites "no free-text chat history", and states the screen-context rule. Plan 03 extends "The tutor during practice" with the live agent and the post-submission worked-solution discussion. Plan 13 gains the streaming-with-screen rule, the measured per-turn cache behaviour, and the template rules for the new templates. Plan 14 gains the caps and cost lines. Plan 08's "no chat box beside an unsolved problem" becomes "no unguarded chat, none open by default". Plan 12 gets the rulings below.

## Where the strands disagreed, and how it was resolved [inferred]

| Question | Strand positions | Resolution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Long-lived process or per-turn process | providers: long-lived first, per-turn rejected for start-up cost and cache loss | Measured: the per-turn path caches the system prefix and starts in about 1 s. Per-turn wins on simplicity and on fitting the guard |
| Model for consolidation | memory: `claude-haiku-4-5`; providers: Haiku retires 15 October 2026 | `claude-sonnet-5-5` for both new roles |
| Separate profile role | self-tuning: its own `tutor_profile` role; memory: a `memory` role | One `memory` role and one job; the profile's two extracted fields join the consolidation schema |
| How long turns are kept | memory: a buffer deleted within 7 days; ux: readable and deletable in settings | 30 days, readable and deletable, with a ruling requested |
| Ollama offline floor | providers: optional, scope limited to submitted items; brief: research it as the floor | Not built; the panel's offline state is copy, not a model. Ruling requested with the measurement named |
| Memory in the cached prefix | plan 07 puts the misconception list in the prefix; every strand keeps student-derived text below the marker | Below the marker, uncached, as the template already does. Plan 07's sentence is corrected in the amendment |

## Rulings that need the operator [uncertain]

- Whether a minor who is not the subscription holder may converse through the operator's Claude login. The consumer terms require users to be 18 or older and bar making the account available to others (`providers.md`). The design does not change the answer, only the volume of calls.
- Whether the operator's Claude model-improvement setting is off. On the subscription path it decides whether the student's words may be kept up to 5 years (`providers.md`).
- Whether conversation turns are kept 30 days (this synthesis) or deleted within 7 days of consolidation (`memory.md`).
- Whether consolidation may also read `attempts.error_note` and `attempts.self_explanation` from sessions where the agent was never opened (`memory.md`).
- Whether to build the Ollama floor, and if so on which model, after measuring memory, speed and accuracy on a sample of BC items (`providers.md`).
- The plan 09 number for KaTeX `maxExpand` on model output, since "macro expansion disabled" needs a value that still covers `\lim` (`live-assistant-ux.md`).
- Whether Ctrl+/ works on the operator's keyboard layouts other than US (`live-assistant-ux.md`).
