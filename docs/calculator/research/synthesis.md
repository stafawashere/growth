# Desmos fluency, research synthesis

Inputs: [bluebook-desmos.md](bluebook-desmos.md) (which calculator, what it does, the API and the terms), [exam-calculator-work.md](exam-calculator-work.md) (what the calculator parts ask for and how readers score it), [desmos-technique.md](desmos-technique.md) (the documented keystrokes), [fluency-training.md](fluency-training.md) (how tool fluency is trained and displayed). ## Decisions the evidence supports, ranked [inferred]

1. **Drill the College Board version of the Desmos graphing calculator, not the public one.** Calculus BC gets the graphing calculator and only that (web-calc-policy p.1, desmos-help-assessment-faq p.1, desmos-cb-calculators-pdf p.1). Desmos says testing calculators may differ from desmos.com and that its Testing page carries the configuration a test taker meets (desmos-help-testing-calculators p.1); for SY2026-2027 the College Board graphing calculator differs only by disabled images, folders and notes and by automatic log mode on certain regressions (desmos-cb-calculators-pdf p.1). The practice URL redirects to https://www.desmos.com/testing/collegeboard/graphing, same origin as the public calculator, so the CSP's `frame-src https://www.desmos.com` covers it unchanged. Weakest point: no College Board page says the practice page is the build Bluebook embeds.
2. **Check outcomes, not the graph state.** The exam scores two things per calculator result, the written setup and the three-place value, and every cached guideline year scores them separately (exam-calculator-work.md, The setup-plus-result rule). Both are outcomes the student produces outside the calculator, so a drill that reads the typed setup and the typed value measures what the reader measures without seeing inside Desmos. The API route that would see inside (getState, observeEvent) needs an API key from an account only the operator can create and a `script-src` amendment plan 09 refuses (bluebook-desmos.md, The Desmos API and its terms). Not adopted; recorded as a ruling.
3. **Accept rounded and truncated three-place values, and say which the student gave.** sg-25 p.2 and p.3 state the three-place rule and that rounding or truncation both earn (exam-calculator-work.md, Three decimals). The drill's value check therefore accepts either form and shows both, so the student learns the rule as the reader applies it, and a value with fewer than three places is refused with the reason, since that is the form the reader refuses (BC-ERR-02032, BC-ERR-99019).
4. **Score the setup as a written expression, equivalence-checked, never as keystrokes.** The habit the exam pays for is the setup on the page (ced p.13; "Show the setup for your calculations" in the 2023 to 2026 stems, exam-calculator-work.md), and a bare solved value earned nothing in 2025 (sg-25 p.4, p.5, p.9). The Desmos keystrokes are the means; the app checks the written setup by MathJSON equivalence through the grader the items already use.
5. **Radians first, as a card and as a note on every trigonometric drill.** The radian parenthetical is in both 2025 and 2026 calculator questions and degree-mode notes in the 2021 to 2023 guidelines (exam-calculator-work.md, Radian-mode cues); Desmos states that testing calculators often default to degrees (desmos-help-testing-calculators p.1) even though the College Board page opened in radians on one observation. The first card is the angle-mode check and its toggle shortcut (desmos-graphing-shortcuts p.1).
6. **Rank the drill capabilities by the indexed frequency of the work.** Definite integrals in 34 of 86 indexed calculator parts across all 22 questions, derivatives at a point in 27, numerical solving in 20 (exam-calculator-work.md, Implications; denominators stated there). The drilled capabilities are integral (including average value and an initial value plus an integral), derivative at a point, zeros and f(x)=k, intersection as a bound, value at a point, and a window that exposes an extreme value, which together cover the four CED capabilities and the exam's three further uses.
7. **Solve by points of interest, never by tracing.** Desmos documents zeros and intersections only as gray points of interest with coordinates (desmos-help-getting-started p.1, desmos-help-faqs p.1), and the 2024 Chief Reader ties lost third decimals to tracing (cr-24 p.28). The zeros and intersection cards teach the click, the label and Export Point to Expression List.
8. **Record time and accuracy separately and show the student's own median with denominators, no target.** Speed is traded for accuracy under any speed emphasis (Heitz 2014) and individual practice curves are exponential, so cohort norms mislead (Heathcote et al 2000) (fluency-training.md). The measured view shows the median seconds per drill, the accuracy of the value and of the setup, each over "answered drills", and the exam's own per-question figures from research/exam/exam-structure.md stated as the exam's figures. Plan 15's `fluent` label is not extended to drills, since it compares a median to a budget, which acts as a threshold.
9. **Demonstrate, then drill; one procedure per task; feedback right after.** The worked-example effect and the acquisition-before-fluency ordering (fluency-training.md, Demonstration before practice; Automaticity) put a demonstration on every card and a "Drill this" button after it; deliberate practice on components with informative feedback (Ericsson et al 1993) puts one capability per task and the verdicts immediately after Check.
10. **Mix and space, at the student's choice.** Random practice retains better than blocked (Shea and Morgan 1979) and gains flatten within a session (Heathcote et al 2000), so the chooser offers a mixed draw across capabilities and the drill stops when the student stops; nothing schedules a return, because a schedule is a quota.

## Rejected alternatives [inferred]

| Alternative | Why rejected | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| The app's own graphing panel as the drill calculator | It is not Desmos; a student who rehearses another tool rehearses another strategy (plan 05, Timed section drill). The Bluebook variant is a full graphing Desmos, so the panel is under-featured | bluebook-desmos.md, What this settles |
| The embedded Desmos API (calculator.js) | Needs an operator-created API key and a `script-src` change plan 09 refuses; the Trial Tier licence itself permits personal non-commercial use, so licence is not the blocker | bluebook-desmos.md, The Desmos API and its terms |
| The public desmos.com/calculator as the drill frame | Differs from the exam configuration (images, folders, notes, log mode); Desmos points test takers at the testing page | desmos-cb-calculators-pdf p.1, desmos-help-testing-calculators p.1 |
| Checking the student's Desmos keystrokes | Not observable without the API, and not what the reader scores | exam-calculator-work.md |
| A blended fluency score, a pass mark, a rate aim or a `fluent` label for drills | A target; speed and accuracy trade, so a blend hides the trade | fluency-training.md, Rejected mechanics |
| Streaks, badges, leaderboards, daily goals, praise for speed | Goals and praise, forbidden by the project rules; the literature shows fixation and speed pressure | fluency-training.md, Rejected mechanics |
| A running clock hidden from the student | Bluebook shows a hideable timer; the drill mirrors it, hideable, so the exam's own instrument is rehearsed | web-bluebook-tools p.1 |
| Reproducing released calculator stems as drill tasks | Copyright rule of the library; the tasks are drawn from templates | CLAUDE.md |
| Tracing the curve as a technique | Loses third decimals; not documented by Desmos as the way to read coordinates | cr-24 p.28, desmos-help-getting-started p.1 |

## Rulings for the operator [inferred]

1. **Framing Desmos and the desmos.com terms.** The desmos.com Terms of Service, section 5, state "You may not frame or mirror the Desmos Tools without our prior consent" (desmos-terms p.1). The app has framed desmos.com/calculator since the operator's instruction of 2026-09-27, and this feature frames the College Board version the same way. The feature is built so the frame is one constant away from a new-window link (architecture.md, The Desmos frame), and the operator should rule whether to seek Desmos's consent, keep the frame for single-user private use, or switch the default to the new-window link. No cached page says which terms govern the College Board testing page.
2. **The Desmos API.** If the operator obtains an API key at desmos.com/my-api under the Trial Tier and rules that the CSP may name desmos.com for scripts, the frame can be replaced by the API calculator with the graph state held in memory only, and the setup check can additionally compare the typed Desmos expression. Not done here.
3. **Re-fetch before May 2027.** The approved calculator list and the College Board Desmos PDF both say they are updated yearly; both should be re-fetched in the spring of 2027 (tools/fetch_corpus.py web with --force).
