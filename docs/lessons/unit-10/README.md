### The series free-response question and the points it scores

The BC series free-response question is Q6 in each year read, no calculator, Section II Part B; across 2021 to 2025 it opened with an interval or radius of convergence part in 2021, 2022 and 2025, a convergence test at an endpoint in 2024, and a Taylor polynomial construction in 2023 (research/units/unit-10-infinite-sequences-series.md#Archetype summary). The part shapes and what each point is for, from the archetype `scoring_pattern` and the BC-PT `earns` and `does_not_earn` fields:

- Interval of convergence (BC-QA-10013, five points; four when both endpoints take one test, sg-22:20). Ratio point BC-PT-99042: a correct ratio of consecutive terms, with or without absolute values; no ratio leaves the limit point unavailable (sg-25:25). Limit point BC-PT-99043: the limit evaluated with limit notation; any simplification or evaluation error forfeits it while the ratio point stands (sg-25:25). Interior point BC-PT-99044: the inequality resolved to a two-sided inequality or interval; the bare absolute value form is not sufficient (sg-25:25). Endpoints point BC-PT-99045: both endpoints substituted and both series written; one endpoint only fails (sg-22:21). Analysis and interval point BC-PT-99046: each endpoint analysed, naming an appropriate test is sufficient, plus the interval stated explicitly; an interval with no endpoint analysis, or analysis from a reciprocal ratio, fails (sg-25:25). A reciprocal ratio keeps every other point reachable, and an interior that earned its point but is wrong still supports the endpoint point (sg-25:25).
- Radius of convergence (BC-QA-10014, three points): ratio BC-PT-99042, limit BC-PT-99043 (requires the ratio point first), radius BC-PT-99047 stated explicitly; an interval presented without naming the radius fails (sg-21:24). After term-by-term differentiation the radius point rests on the preservation statement (BC-QA-10018, sg-24:22).
- Named test at a number or an endpoint (BC-QA-10004, BC-QA-10015, two points): one point for considering the right series, one for the answer with a reason, BC-PT-99005; a conclusion with no supporting condition earns the first point only (sg-24:19); the reason point is lost when the test named does not fit the series (sg-24:20).
- Integral test (BC-QA-10005, three points): all three conditions for the first; the improper integral for the second, which an incorrect lower limit costs while leaving the third reachable; the evaluation with limit notation for the third, BC-PT-99053, which an evaluation written with the infinity symbol does not earn (sg-21:21, sg-21:22).
- Limit comparison and absolute convergence (BC-QA-10006, BC-QA-10007, two points): setup with limit notation (BC-PT-99042 on the record), then the explanation, which requires the limit to be positive and finite, absolute value symbols explicit or implicit, and a conclusion naming the series; the reciprocal quotient is accepted (sg-21:23).
- Taylor polynomial (BC-QA-10010, four points): form of the product rule BC-PT-99022, the derivative BC-PT-99027, first two terms BC-PT-99035, remaining terms BC-PT-99036; a nonzero term of the wrong degree or a trailing ellipsis loses the final point (sg-23:19). A correct expanded form not in powers of the centre earns BC-PT-99035 but not BC-PT-99036 (sg-25:22). For a related function (BC-QA-10012, three points): the derivative relation, the first two terms, the complete polynomial; a coefficient with no support does not earn the final point, and an alternate solution through known Maclaurin series earns the same three (sg-23:20, sg-23:21).
- A series written from a known one (BC-QA-10016, 10017, 10018, 10019): first nonzero terms BC-PT-99037 and general term BC-PT-99038 scored separately, terms accepted in a list or as part of a series (sg-25:26); an ellipsis with no closed form for the nth term fails BC-PT-99038 (sg-22:22). A geometric closed form to verify earns BC-PT-99068 on an unsimplified first term over one minus ratio (sg-25:26); BC-PT-99067 needs a ratio of the correct magnitude (sg-26:21).
- Error bounds (BC-QA-10008, BC-QA-10009, two points each). Alternating series bound: BC-PT-99040 for the first omitted term evaluated at the input, where a term of the wrong degree or a term listed inside a polynomial fails (sg-22:21); BC-PT-99041 for the justification, which requires the alternating, decreasing, limit-zero conditions and an inequality against the target; an equality claim fails (sg-22:21, sg-24:21). A single-point form asks only for an upper bound (sg-21:25). Lagrange bound: BC-PT-99039 for the form, maximum of the next derivative over the interval divided by the factorial times the distance to the matching power; the wrong factorial or power fails (sg-23:20); BC-PT-99041 for the inequality against the tolerance, which later simplification errors cost while the form point stays (sg-23:20).
- Convergence to the function at an input (BC-QA-10020, one point, BC-PT-99005): the answer with a reason placing the input relative to the interval; the point is available with an imported wrong interval, and a geometric ratio argument is accepted (sg-25:27).

Justification is the thread through every part: the named test's conditions, the endpoint tests and the error-bound conditions are each a point or a condition of one (research/scoring/justification-requirements.md#Justification inside series work).
