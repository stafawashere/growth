# Rulings waiting on the operator

Written by claude-fable-5-1 on the operator's delegation during the overnight lesson redesign of 2026-09-29. Nothing here was adopted. Each entry names the technique, the products that use it, the rule it hits (CLAUDE.md, plan 01, plan 08, plan 15 or `tools/check_lessons.py`), the reasoning for and against, and the ruling the run took by default (always the rule).

## Techniques rejected under the ground rules [inferred]

| Id | Technique | Products | Rule it hits | Reasoning | Default taken |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Daily streak with freeze days | Duolingo (7-day streak share up almost three times, company-reported), Brilliant (3 problems or 1 lesson a day, Streak Charge), IXL (days-practised achievements) | Plan 01 "Implementation intention and queue-bound streak" allows only a streak whose unit is a completed due queue; plan 08 anti-pattern "streak-loss guilt notifications"; CLAUDE.md "no study advice, no schedules" | The measured gains are engagement, not learning; plan 01 already carries a queue-bound streak that no lesson touches. A lesson-reading streak would reward reading, which plan 15 L0 says counts toward nothing | No streak on lessons |
| R2 | XP, points and leagues | Duolingo, Brilliant (weekly leagues of 30), Math Academy (XP as minutes, leagues), Alcumus (XP, quests, achievements), IXL (medals) | Plan 01 "What the evidence does not support": no badges, no points for volume, no leaderboards (Deci, Koestner and Ryan 1999, d = -0.36 completion-contingent); plan 08 anti-patterns "leaderboards and leagues" | Math Academy's XP is a minute-of-effort unit, which plan 08 already borrows as the minute forecast on home. Nothing else in the group has learning evidence | No XP, no leagues, no medals |
| R3 | Praise copy, celebratory sound, haptics and flashes on a correct answer, praise at unpredictable intervals | Duolingo | Plan 08 "Interface writing": feedback is informational and never praise; `tools/check_lessons.py` PRAISE lint | Duolingo reports it as engagement design and gives no learning evidence; the Deci meta-analysis places verbal praise at d = +0.33 for free-choice behaviour, which is the one form plan 08 allows only as information about what was demonstrated | "Correct" with its glyph and nothing more |
| R4 | Hearts, energy or lives that stop practice after mistakes | Duolingo | Plan 01 "Mastery gating": progression is gated on evidence, never on a quota; plan 08 "one next action" | A lockout after errors punishes the attempt the lesson exists to elicit; no product reports a learning gain from it | Unlimited attempts on every check |
| R5 | Points lost on a wrong answer, progress reset | IXL SmartScore (3 to 8 points off in the Challenge Zone), ALEKS (1 point off), DeltaMath ("Back to Zero"), Alcumus (topic un-passed) | Plan 15 L0: a lesson check writes nothing to any state; plan 08 "Interface writing": no score on a lesson | The critics of SmartScore are competitors, but IXL's own guide states the asymmetry, and a score that falls turns the check into a verdict on the student. The engine's own credited failures already carry the honest version | No score of any kind on a lesson check |
| R6 | A recommended dose per day or per week | Math Academy (20 to 40 XP on 3 to 5 days), Brilliant (15 minutes a day), Duolingo (5-minute lessons) | CLAUDE.md "No study advice, no schedules, nowhere"; `tools/check_lessons.py` SCHEDULE lint; plan 01 "the product does not set a daily quota" | The only figure with any evidence is Baddeley and Longman's distribution result, which plan 01 records as a finding and not a recommendation | The reader states the lesson's own forecast minutes and nothing about the student's day |
| R7 | A chat tutor beside the lesson | Khanmigo (GPT-4, "avoids giving direct answers"), Brilliant's Koji ("guides the learner through the reasoning") | Plan 01 "What the evidence does not support" and plan 08 anti-pattern "chat-first layout" (Bastani et al, 17 percent below control on the unassisted exam); plan 15 "no tutor call runs inside the reader" | The guardrailed tutor reached parity at best. Neither product publishes an error rate | No chat in the reader; the fix prompt and the retry carry the interaction |
| R8 | Hints one at a time, the last hint giving the answer | Khan Academy (an authoring rule) | Plan 01 "Feedback timing": the answer is never visible before commitment; plan 15 Interface writing | A hint ladder ending in the answer is a solution on demand, which Photomath's file shows invites copying | No hints; a wrong answer shows the right step after the attempt |
| R9 | A mastery percent or a progress bar that fills | Khan (course mastery percent), Brilliant (course progress), IXL (SmartScore 0 to 100) | Plan 08 anti-pattern "percentage-complete bars on the mastery graph"; plan 15 UI: "no percent bar and no count of lessons read" | Retention decays and reading is not mastery; the library shows a state per lesson instead | The within-lesson part counter stays ("Part 3 of 12"), because it counts screens of one lesson, not mastery |
| R10 | A predicted grade level or score | IXL (grade-level number from the diagnostic) | CLAUDE.md "no predict-talk"; plan 05 and plan 08 "Mock score" row | The exam's cut points are unpublished, so any number is a prediction the app cannot support | The band with its assumptions, as plan 05 already shows |
| R11 | Explanatory video as a delivery mode | 3Blue1Brown | Not a rule conflict. Plan 15 caps a full lesson at 6 minutes and plan 01 keeps at least 70 percent of session time on attempted problems | The Essence of Calculus chapters run 13:50 to 17:34, Guo 2014 finds engagement falls past six minutes, Muller 2008 finds a clear explanation can leave reasoning unchanged, and no narration policy or recording pipeline exists. The two techniques the form carries, the puzzle-first hook and continuous transformation, are taken as the prediction screen and the existing motion mode | No video mode; recorded here so the operator can overrule it |
| R12 | Implicit-only concept introduction with no stated rule | Duolingo ("no lecture or explicit explanation"), Alcumus (a problem and a solution, no lesson) | Plan 15 Content model: every lesson carries a key idea block sourced to the CED | Duolingo publishes no efficacy study for its math course, and Devlin's criticism (procedural knowledge without comprehension) is the cost. The prediction screen keeps the attempt-first benefit while the key idea still states the rule | The rule is always stated, after the prediction |

## Caps the new framework designed inside, and would have wanted loosened [inferred]

No cap was changed. Each row states what the framework does within the cap and what a loosening would buy, so the operator can weigh it.

| Id | Cap | Value | What the framework does inside it | Proposed loosening and reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | `LESSON_CHECKS_MAX` | 3 | The prediction, the fix prompts on error blocks and the faded second example are response prompts on sections, stored in `lesson_check_responses` like a check but not counted as checks, so the three checks stay completion, isomorph and multiple choice | None needed. Recorded because a reader of plan 15 might count the prompts as checks; the record and the schema keep them apart on purpose |
| C2 | `LESSON_WORDS_FULL_MAX` and `LESSON_READ_MINUTES_MAX` | 900 words, 6 minutes | The prediction screen (at most 40 words) and the contrast pair (at most 60 words) fit because designers trim extended key ideas first; the measured full-band median before the redesign was 481 words, the maximum 825 | A raise to 1,000 words would let the four longest lessons keep every extended key idea beside the new screens. Not proposed as needed; listed so the operator knows which lessons were trimmed (the ledger names them) |
| C3 | `CUE_WORDS_MAX` on a strategy cue | 20 | The contrast pair's stems are separate fields with their own 30-word cap, so the cue stays at 20 | None |
| C4 | `LESSON_BRIEF_MINUTES_MAX` | 3 minutes | The brief form gains the prediction screen and the contrast pair, and 22 lessons sat at exactly 3.0 minutes before the redesign, so those designs drop the second error block from the brief form to fit | A raise to 3.5 minutes would keep both error blocks in the brief form on those lessons. Not proposed as needed |
