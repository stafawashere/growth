# Synthesis

This file reads across `docs/pedagogy/products/*.md` and `docs/pedagogy/learning-science.md`. A technique is listed when at least two products use it or one product uses it and the science file carries evidence for it. Each row names the products, the evidence, the tag the evidence earns, the fit to Growth (an AP Calculus BC tutor in which lessons, checks and refreshers count toward no mastery evidence, plan 15 L0), and the decision the redesign takes. Product claims are tagged as the product files tag them; most are [single-source], because vendors describe themselves and few independent measurements exist.

## How the products divide [inferred]

The twelve subjects fall into four groups, and the group decides what each can teach Growth.

| Group | Products | What they are for | What Growth takes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adaptive mastery tutors | Math Academy, ALEKS, Khan Academy, IXL | Sequence a curriculum by a knowledge model and gate progress on evidence | The lesson dose (explain, example, practice at once), the outer-fringe idea Growth already has, mastery confirmed by later retrieval |
| Interaction-first lessons | Brilliant, Duolingo Math, Desmos Classroom | Make the student commit to a prediction or a manipulation before the rule is stated | Prediction before instruction, manipulable figures with a question attached, interpretive feedback |
| Practice engines | DeltaMath, AoPS Alcumus | Serve generated problems and a worked solution after the attempt | Attempt before solution, the solution in the student's own values, a second try before the answer |
| Reference forms | Photomath, explanatory video, Anki and FSRS | Explain one problem, explain one idea, or schedule recall | Expandable steps with a why per step, the puzzle-first hook, and the spacing model Growth already runs |

No product in the set teaches AP Calculus BC by evidence of learning outcomes. The efficacy studies that exist are K-8 (Khan, 350,000 students, grades 3 to 8; IXL, 545 students, grades 3 to 5, effect size 0.13), vendor-published (Amplify Desmos, ALEKS case studies, Duolingo engagement numbers), or small quasi-experiments (Brilliant, 60 students; Photomath, 60 and 46 students). ALEKS is the only product with an independent meta-analysis (Fang et al 2019, 15 studies), and it found ALEKS as good as, not better than, traditional teaching. So the ranking below leans on the learning-science file, and product practice is read as existence proof and interaction design, not as outcome evidence.

## Techniques ranked by evidence and fit [inferred]

The evidence column cites the learning-science file section and the strongest effect it carries. Fit is judged against plan 15's constraints: no mastery credit for lessons, a 6-minute and 900-word cap on the full form, a 3-minute and 450-word cap on the brief form, no praise, no study advice, no schedule, no predict-talk, facts from the library only.

## What the products do badly, read as warnings [single-source]

Each line names the product file that records it and the Growth rule that already guards against it, or the rule the redesign adds.

- Points that fall on a wrong answer (IXL SmartScore drops 3 to 8 in the Challenge Zone, ALEKS costs a point, DeltaMath "Back to Zero", Alcumus un-passes a topic). Growth: a lesson check writes nothing to any mastery state (plan 15 L0), and the verdict carries no score.
- The last hint gives the answer (Khan's authoring rule) and hint use marks the question wrong. Growth: no hints on a lesson check; a wrong answer shows the right step and offers one retry.
- Pattern matching on multiple choice (Math Academy reviewers, Khan's engineer as cited by Meyer, Duolingo's implicit-only introduction). Growth: check 1 and check 2 are generation, only check 3 is multiple choice, and its distractors each carry an error path.
- Thin explanations with no motivation (Math Academy reviewers, ALEKS "short lessons lack depth"). Growth: the prediction screen supplies the motivation, and the orientation says what a response must show.
- Long unskippable remedial runs after one mistake (Math Academy diagnostic). Growth: a lesson is always skippable and has no engine effect.
- Streaks, XP, leagues, energy and hearts (Duolingo, Brilliant, Math Academy, IXL medals, AoPS achievements). Growth: none; see `rulings-for-operator.md`.
- Engagement measured instead of learning (Duolingo streaks up three times, Guo's watch time). Growth: the layer is judged on minutes to mastery per skill and delayed accuracy (plan 15 Telemetry).
- Answer-format strictness marks correct mathematics wrong (ALEKS, DeltaMath, Alcumus). Growth: `app/items/verify.py equivalence` grades short answers by CAS equivalence.
- A solution with no attempt (Photomath). Growth: no solution before a commitment on any check.
- Dependence on a live teacher for the Connect phase (Desmos). Growth: the contrast pair and the fix prompt carry the comparison inside the lesson.

## Techniques rejected under the ground rules [inferred]

Each is recorded in full, with the rule it hits and the reasoning, in `docs/pedagogy/rulings-for-operator.md`, so the operator can rule later. The list: daily streaks and streak freezes (Duolingo, Brilliant, IXL); XP and leagues (Duolingo, Brilliant, Math Academy, Alcumus); praise copy and celebratory sound, haptics and flashes on a correct answer (Duolingo); hearts and energy limits (Duolingo); points lost on a wrong answer (IXL, ALEKS, DeltaMath, Alcumus); a recommended minutes-per-day or days-per-week target (Math Academy 20 to 40 XP on 3 to 5 days, Brilliant 15 minutes a day); a chat tutor beside the lesson (Khanmigo, Koji); a hint whose last step is the answer (Khan); a mastery percent bar (Khan course mastery percent, Brilliant progress); a predicted score or level (IXL grade-level number).

## The techniques Growth keeps that no product in the set has [inferred]

Three things in the current framework have no counterpart in the twelve products and are kept: the scoring checklist built from BC-PT records, because the criterion is an AP rubric and no product in the set teaches to one; the strategy block's cue, method and rival taken verbatim from the archetype record, because recognition is what the exam pays for; and the blind re-solve plus block audit before a lesson is served, because every product above serves vendor-written content with no published verification step.
