# Executive summary

This plan describes an adaptive web application that teaches one student to mastery of every skill the AP Calculus BC exam assesses in its May 2027 form. It is documentation only. The domain model is the research library under `research/` and `data/`, consumed by ID and never re-derived (see [research/README.md](../../research/README.md)). Every decision below is justified by learning impact first, then cost, then convenience, and every number carries a source and one of the tags [verified], [single-source] or [inferred].

## The teaching thesis [inferred from the cited evidence]

The student's minutes are the scarcest resource. The application spends them, in this order, on four things. First, mastery-gated practice at the outer fringe of the 1226-edge prerequisite graph over 541 atomic skills, because mastery learning carries the best-replicated adaptive effect (d = 0.4 to 0.6, Kulik, Kulik and Bangert-Drowns 1990, https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.3102/00346543060002265 [single-source]; median 0.66 for intelligent tutors, shrinking on standardized tests, Kulik and Fletcher 2016, https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.3102/0034654315581420 [verified]). Second, interleaved and spaced retrieval of everything already learned, scheduled to the exam date, because interleaving has the strongest mathematics-specific classroom trial in the ledger (d = 0.83 on an unannounced one-month test, Rohrer, Dedrick, Hartwig and Cheung 2020, http://uweb.cas.usf.edu/~drohrer/pdfs/Rohrer_et_al_2020JEdPsych.pdf [verified]) and practice testing beats restudy across 188 experiments (g = 0.51, Adesope, Trevisan and Sundararajan 2017, https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.3102/0034654316689306 [verified]). Both are reorderings of work the student was going to do anyway, so they cost no extra minutes. Third, worked examples faded adaptively per skill until the skill is acquired, then retrieval only (Salden et al on adaptive fading, Kalyuga et al 2003 on expertise reversal, both [single-source], see [01-learning-model.md](01-learning-model.md)). Fourth, criterion-shaped rehearsal at a fixed low cadence: free-response justification writing graded per scoring point, part-shaped timed drills, and full-length mocks in the 2027 format.

There is no chat box next to an unsolved problem. In the one field experiment on this question, unguarded chat raised practice scores by 48 percent and lowered the unassisted exam score by 17 percent against control, while a guardrailed tutor reached parity (Bastani et al, https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4895486 [single-source]). Guardrails buy parity, not upside, so assistance is a floor and the scheduling mechanics are where the learning comes from.

The plan sets expectations at d = 0.4 to 0.7 against a standardized criterion, not two sigma, and requires measurement against released AP material every six weeks ([01-learning-model.md](01-learning-model.md), [10-quality-and-evaluation.md](10-quality-and-evaluation.md)).

## Mastery model and selection policy

Mastery model: per-skill Performance Factors Analysis with log-scaled success and failure counts, difficulty declared from the library's difficulty factors rather than learned, prerequisite propagation of fractional credit along hard edges, and FSRS-7 with its published default parameters as the scheduler for decay ([02-adaptive-engine.md](02-adaptive-engine.md)). PFA is the only classical model built for multi-skill items (Pavlik, Cen and Koedinger 2009, https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED506305.pdf [verified]), which matters because only 31 of the 541 skills are isolated by a single archetype. Item difficulty cannot be learned from one learner (at least 100 students are needed, Pelanek 2016, https://www.fi.muni.cz/~xpelanek/publications/CAE-elo.pdf [verified]). Deep knowledge tracing is rejected because it overfits even at 574 learners (Gervet et al 2020, https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/EJ1273917.pdf [verified]). FSRS is kept as a scheduler and not as the mastery model because it has no notion of an unacquired skill or a prerequisite, and because a zero-parameter recency baseline beats it on its own benchmark (https://github.com/open-spaced-repetition/srs-benchmark [verified]).

Selection policy: fringe-restricted (no item whose hard prerequisites are unmastered), with a two-term score in the first two phases (due-review coverage, then uniform random within the fringe), hard interleaving constraints on every assembled set, a 30-item capped cold-start diagnostic in the ALEKS style (measures flat after question 10, https://jmatayoshi.github.io/publications/JMP2021_KST_ALEKS_preprint.pdf [verified]), and repetition compression so one downstream item discharges several due reviews (direction corroborated by ALEKS retention by layer, same source [verified]). The five-term adaptive score that the first draft proposed is deferred until offline simulation shows it beats the two-term score, because none of its weights has a source ([02-adaptive-engine.md](02-adaptive-engine.md), [10-quality-and-evaluation.md](10-quality-and-evaluation.md)).

## Stack

Python 3.12 with FastAPI for the engine, grader, SymPy verifier, content loader and provider layer; React with TypeScript and Vite for the client with KaTeX rendering and MathLive input; SQLite through SQLAlchemy first with a Postgres path; a single background worker over a job table; SSE for streaming ([06-architecture.md](06-architecture.md)). The verifier and grader are where learning-critical correctness lives and SymPy is the strongest deterministic checker available, so the whole engine sits in one testable Python process; the split stack costs one extra process. Providers: Anthropic first with prompt caching, Gemini as second tier, Ollama offline fallback, OpenAI and OpenRouter adapters off by default, and a claudebox adapter locked to single-operator self-hosting ([07-ai-provider-layer.md](07-ai-provider-layer.md)).

## Top five risks

1. Cold-start collapse of the conjunctive item prediction. A product of several low per-skill probabilities makes every candidate look equally hard; the plan centres the difficulty prior and gates phase one on a published distribution of predicted probabilities ([02-adaptive-engine.md](02-adaptive-engine.md), [11-phased-delivery.md](11-phased-delivery.md)). The conjunctive split by edge type is the one structural assumption no published model shares.
2. Free-response grading is not human-equivalent. The best rubric-grading agreement in the ledger is kappa 0.56 and question-level exact agreement at most 0.22 on a small study (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.00451 and https://arxiv.org/html/2607.01247 [single-source]). Every point is graded per scoring point with deterministic checks first, sampled for agreement, shown as provisional, and per-point grading is kept out of the daily session ([03-diagnosis-and-feedback.md](03-diagnosis-and-feedback.md), [05-assessment-modes.md](05-assessment-modes.md)).
3. Handwritten capture friction causes free-response avoidance. Transcription, not mathematics, produced 87 percent of residual grading errors in the cited study (https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19043 [single-source]), so the read-back must be confirmed before grading, and a new metric tracks weekly free-response attempts and read-back abandonment ([10-quality-and-evaluation.md](10-quality-and-evaluation.md)).
4. Inferred parameters stacked on inferred parameters. The engine's tunables table has about thirty rows with no source; each is listed with the measurement that would settle it in [12-open-questions.md](12-open-questions.md), and the simulation harness exists to falsify them.
5. Library gaps block parts of the plan. Fifty-six active archetypes have no scoring point types, the archetype parameter specification is prose, and the prerequisite graph carries 48 edges to topic nodes rather than skills. These are listed with owners in [12-open-questions.md](12-open-questions.md) so the research work can be prioritised.

Copyright constraint carried from the ledgers: College Board defines commercial use of its materials to include test-prep settings (https://privacy.collegeboard.org/copyright-trademark/request-instructions [verified]). The application serves no official stem, figure or rubric text; released items are structure only ([04-item-generation.md](04-item-generation.md)).
