# AP Calculus BC Exam Blueprint

Question counts and timings live in [exam-structure.md](exam-structure.md) and are not restated here.

## Unit weighting on the multiple-choice section [verified]

Source: BC-SRC-ced p.199 (PDF p.204).

The CED publishes unit weightings for the multiple-choice section only. No unit weighting is published for the free-response section. The AB column is reproduced alongside the BC column because the two courses share Units 1 through 8 and because the AB subscore is built from shared content.

| Unit | Title | AB weighting | BC weighting |
|---|---|---|---|
| BC-UNIT-01 | Limits and Continuity | 10 to 15% | 5 to 10% |
| BC-UNIT-02 | Differentiation: Definition and Fundamental Properties | 10 to 15% | 5 to 10% |
| BC-UNIT-03 | Differentiation: Composite, Implicit, and Inverse Functions | 5 to 10% | 5 to 10% |
| BC-UNIT-04 | Contextual Applications of Differentiation | 10 to 15% | 5 to 10% |
| BC-UNIT-05 | Applying Derivatives to Analyze Functions | 15 to 20% | 10 to 15% |
| BC-UNIT-06 | Integration and Accumulation of Change | 15 to 20% | 15 to 20% |
| BC-UNIT-07 | Differential Equations | 5 to 10% | 5 to 10% |
| BC-UNIT-08 | Applications of Integration | 10 to 15% | 5 to 10% |
| BC-UNIT-09 | Parametric Equations, Polar Coordinates, and Vector-Valued Functions | not in AB | 10 to 15% |
| BC-UNIT-10 | Infinite Sequences and Series | not in AB | 15 to 20% |

The CED marks Units 9 and 10 "BC ONLY". AB is assessed over eight units and BC over ten. Every BC band is a range, so the table constrains rather than fixes the composition of any one form.

## Big ideas assessed [verified]

Source: BC-SRC-ced p.198 (PDF p.203); BC-SRC-ced p.13 (PDF p.18).

The exam assesses content from three big ideas: Change (CHA), Limits (LIM), and Analysis of Functions (FUN). The CED attaches series to Big Idea 2 with a "bc only" marker. No weighting is published by big idea; weighting is published by unit and by practice only.

## Practice weighting on the multiple-choice section [verified]

Source: BC-SRC-ced p.200 (PDF p.205).

| Practice | Title | MC weighting |
|---|---|---|
| BC-MP-1 | Implementing Mathematical Processes | 50 to 70% |
| BC-MP-2 | Connecting Representations | 15 to 30% |
| BC-MP-3 | Justification | 10 to 20% |
| BC-MP-4 | Communication and Notation | not assessed |

The CED states directly that Practice 4 is not assessed in the multiple-choice section. The same page records that both exams include algebraic, exponential, logarithmic, trigonometric, and general types of functions, and analytical, graphical, tabular, and verbal representations.

## The three common free-response questions [verified]

Source: BC-SRC-ced p.201 (PDF p.206); BC-SRC-sg-25 p.2.

The AB and BC exams share three free-response questions, which the CED describes as assessing content from the domain of the AB course. The shared questions are printed on both forms and are scored against one rubric. The 2025 scoring guidelines mark shared questions with the header "Part A (AB or BC)" or "Part B (AB or BC)", while BC-exclusive questions are headed for BC alone. The CED adds that both exams include a roughly equal mix of procedural and conceptual tasks, various function types and representations, and at least two questions that incorporate a real-world context or scenario.

## How the AB subscore is formed [single-source]

Source: BC-SRC-web-ab-subscore; BC-SRC-ced p.201 (PDF p.206).

A student who takes the BC exam receives two reported scores: a BC score of 1 to 5 and a Calculus AB subscore of 1 to 5. The AB subscore is computed from the portion of the BC exam that covers AB content, which the web source characterises as roughly 60 percent of the exam. That portion comprises the AB-domain multiple-choice questions plus the three common free-response questions described above. The subscore is placed on the same 1 to 5 reporting scale as a standalone AB score and is reported to colleges alongside the BC score.

The CED itself does not describe the subscore. The only cached corroboration that a subscore exists is the annual distribution PDF series (BC-SRC-absub-23, BC-SRC-absub-24, BC-SRC-absub-25), whose footnote records that the count of students with subscores may differ slightly from the count who took the BC exam because of exam administration incidents. Distributions are tabulated in [scoring-system.md](scoring-system.md).

## Unresolved [uncertain]

Source: BC-SRC-ced p.201 (PDF p.206).

- The free-response practice weighting rows are reconstructed from list order in the cached text, not from an intact table. The pairing of BC-MP-2 with 10 to 20% and BC-MP-3 with 35 to 60% is not independently confirmed.
- No unit weighting is published for the free-response section, so unit coverage of Section II cannot be stated from any cached source.
- The "roughly 60 percent" figure for the AB subscore comes from one web page. Neither the exact set of multiple-choice questions counted toward the subscore, nor the raw point total of the subscore, is published.
- Whether the AB-domain multiple-choice questions counted toward the subscore are identical items to those on the AB form, or merely AB-domain items unique to the BC form, is not established.
- The CED does not state whether the three common free-response questions occupy fixed positions within the six.
- Unit weighting bands are ranges and the CED does not state how a single form is constrained within them.
