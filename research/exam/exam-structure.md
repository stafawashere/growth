# AP Calculus BC Exam Structure

This is the only file in the library permitted to state question counts and timings. All other files reference it by link.

## Section and part layout [verified]

Sources: BC-SRC-ced p.198 (PDF p.203); BC-SRC-web-bc-exam.

The exam has two sections. Section I is multiple choice, Section II is free response. Each section is split into two parts that differ in calculator status. The layout below is the structure of the May 2027 administration, the first under the revised multiple-choice counts described in [historical-changes.md](historical-changes.md).

| Section | Part | Question type | Questions | Timing | Calculator | Exam weighting |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | A | Multiple choice | 29 | 62 minutes | Not permitted | 35% |
| I | B | Multiple choice | 13 | 38 minutes | Required | 15% |
| II | A | Free response | 2 | 30 minutes | Required | 16.7% |
| II | B | Free response | 4 | 60 minutes | Not permitted | 33.3% |

Totals: 42 multiple-choice questions, 6 free-response questions, 3 hours and 10 minutes of testing time. Section I carries 50 percent of the exam weight and Section II carries 50 percent.

The two calculator-required parts are Section I Part B and Section II Part A. The two no-calculator parts are Section I Part A and Section II Part B. The CED states the same split in its Unit 1 opener, that two sections of the exam do not allow calculator use and that some questions on the other two require it (BC-SRC-ced p.29, PDF p.34). Calculator consequences are covered in [calculator-policy.md](calculator-policy.md).

## Ordering and internal sequence [verified]

Sources: BC-SRC-ced p.200 (PDF p.205); BC-SRC-ced p.198 (PDF p.203).

Section I is a single numbered run of 42 questions. The CED describes Part B as "the final 13 questions" of Section I, so numbering is continuous across the calculator boundary rather than restarting. Section II is numbered 1 through 6, with the two calculator-required questions first. The scoring guidelines reproduce this order, labelling Question 1 as "Part A (AB or BC): Graphing calculator required" (BC-SRC-sg-25 p.2).

## Free-response point totals [verified]

Sources: BC-SRC-stats-23 p.1; BC-SRC-stats-24 p.1; BC-SRC-stats-25 p.1; BC-SRC-sg-25 p.2.

Each of the six free-response questions carries 9 possible points, for 54 free-response points. The 2023, 2024, and 2025 scoring statistics each list 9 possible points for every one of the six questions, and the 2025 scoring guidelines head Question 1 with "9 points". The number of parts per question varies; point totals per part are set per question in the scoring guidelines. Per-question means are tabulated in [scoring-system.md](scoring-system.md).

## Digital delivery format [single-source]

Source: BC-SRC-web-bc-students.

The exam is administered in a hybrid digital format. Multiple-choice questions are answered in the Bluebook application. Free-response prompts are viewed in Bluebook, and free-response answers are handwritten in paper booklets. The section and part boundaries, and the calculator status of each part, are unchanged by the delivery format.

## Administration date [single-source]

Source: BC-SRC-web-exam-dates.

The AP Calculus BC Exam for the 2027 administration is scheduled for Monday, May 10, 2027, in Session 1. This is a single web source checked on 2026-09-19 and is not corroborated by a cached College Board PDF.

## Accessible technology [verified]

Source: BC-SRC-ced p.198 (PDF p.203).

The CED states that accessible technology with the capabilities expected for AP Calculus is available for students who are blind or visually impaired, and that use on the exam requires an accommodation request through College Board Services for Students with Disabilities. This paragraph is one of the Fall 2026 clarifications, recorded in [historical-changes.md](historical-changes.md).

## Unresolved [uncertain]

Source: BC-SRC-web-bc-exam.

- The CED lists Section II Part A at 16.7% and Part B at 33.3%, which are the free-response section's 50% split by question count rather than by points. Whether the six questions are equally weighted in raw points, given all six carry 9 points, is consistent with the percentages but is not stated as such in any cached source.
- Whether multiple-choice numbering is literally continuous 1 through 42 in the Bluebook interface, as opposed to the printed CED convention, is not established by any cached source.
- The 2027 administration date rests on a single web source and no cached PDF.
- Section I Part A at 62 minutes for 29 questions and Part B at 38 minutes for 13 questions are the revised figures. No cached source states the rationale for the revised per-question pacing.
