# AP Calculus Mathematical Practices and Skills

The practices and skills below are shared by AP Calculus AB and AP Calculus BC. Their weighting by exam section is in [exam-blueprint.md](exam-blueprint.md).

## What the practices are [verified]

Source: BC-SRC-ced p.11 (PDF p.16).

The CED defines the mathematical practices as descriptions of what a student should be able to do while exploring course concepts. The practices are categorised into skills, which the CED states "form the basis of the tasks on the AP Exam". The unit guides embed and spiral these skills through the course, and the CED notes that course content may be paired with a variety of skills on the AP Exam, so no topic is permanently bound to the skill suggested for it in a unit guide.

There are four practices and twenty-three skills. Skill codes take the form practice number, dot, letter. Identifiers in this library are deterministic. A practice takes the prefix BC-MP followed by its number, so Practice 3 is BC-MP-3. A skill takes the prefix BC-MPS followed by its code with the dot removed, so skill 3.D is BC-MPS-3D.

Practice 3 is the largest of the four, with seven skills. Its internal sequence 3.B, 3.C, 3.D is the select, verify, apply chain that the CED's Unit 1 opener singles out, advising explicit practice at first demonstrating that all conditions or hypotheses have been met and then drawing the conclusion (BC-SRC-ced p.29, PDF p.34).

## Assessment status summary [verified]

Source: BC-SRC-ced p.12 (PDF p.17); BC-SRC-ced p.200 (PDF p.205); BC-SRC-ced p.201 (PDF p.206).

Three skills carry the "(not assessed)" marker in the skills table: BC-MPS-1A, BC-MPS-1B, and BC-MPS-3A. All three concern activities that leave no written trace in a response, namely identifying the question, identifying relevant information, and using technology to develop conjectures. The remaining twenty skills carry no marker.

A second layer of exclusion applies at the section level. BC-MP-4 and all five of its skills are not assessed in the multiple-choice section, which the CED states directly. All four practices are assessed in the free-response section. Combining the two rules, BC-MPS-4A through BC-MPS-4E are free-response only.

## How skills attach to content [verified]

Source: BC-SRC-ced p.24 (PDF p.29); BC-SRC-ced p.30 (PDF p.35); BC-SRC-ced p.218 (PDF p.223).

Each CED topic page carries a Suggested Skills field naming one skill, and each unit opener carries a Building the Mathematical Practices block naming a small set of skills for emphasis in that unit, for instance 2.B, 2.C, 3.C, and 3.D for Unit 1. The CED is explicit that these are one way to integrate the skills and that content may be paired with a variety of skills on the exam.

On the assessment side the attachment is finer. Sample multiple-choice questions carry one skill code each in the CED answer table, while free-response scoring points carry one or more skill codes per point, for example a point tagged 2.B alone, a point tagged 3.F and 4.B, and a point tagged 1.D and 4.C within a single question. Skill coding of individual scoring points is the mechanism by which BC-MP-4 earns its 10 to 25 percent of the free-response section without any question being about notation.

## Unresolved [uncertain]

Source: BC-SRC-ced p.12 (PDF p.17).

- The skill texts above are transcribed from an automated text extraction of a multi-column CED table. Column order is unambiguous for the skill codes, but the 4.C example required reconstruction from spelled-out mathematical characters and has not been checked against the rendered PDF.
- The CED does not define what "(not assessed)" excludes: whether the marked skills are absent from item coding entirely or merely absent from published weightings is not stated.
- The practice-level statements for Practices 1 and 2 appear transposed in the cached extraction relative to their column headings; the pairing used here follows the CED's numbering of the skills beneath each column.
- No cached source gives the distribution of skills within a practice's weighting band, so nothing is known about the relative frequency of, for instance, BC-MPS-1C against BC-MPS-1E.
- Whether the Suggested Skills field on a topic page has any bearing on item coding for that topic is not established.
