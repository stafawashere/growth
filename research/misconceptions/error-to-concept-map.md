# Error to Concept Map, AP Calculus BC

One row per active error record in `data/errors.json`, grouped by the unit block of the error id. The possible misconceptions column is many to many in both directions: one error can sit under several misconceptions and one misconception can surface as several errors. Retired records are not listed here; their content moved into the canonical record named by `superseded_by`.

## How to read the table for diagnosis [inferred]

Read a row from left to right and stop at the first column that the evidence supports. The error id and the observed behaviour are what a reader saw. The skills and concepts are what the behaviour touches. The possible misconceptions are candidates, and the non-conceptual causes are their competitors.

The alternative-explanation rule governs the last two of those columns. A misconception may be named only when every entry in the non-conceptual causes cell has been ruled out for the response in hand, and when the same behaviour appears more than once so that a slip is not a plausible account. Where the error appears once and a non-conceptual cause is available, the correct output of the diagnosis is the error id alone.

When more than one misconception is listed, they are rivals for the same observation and the row does not choose between them. The choice comes from the `discriminating_probe` field on each misconception in `data/misconceptions.json` and from the `distinguish_by` field on the matching signals in `data/diagnostic_signals.json`. Until such a probe has been run, the row supports the statement that the behaviour is consistent with the listed misconceptions and supports no statement stronger than that.

Rows whose official evidence notes cell is populated carry Chief Reader citations and are the only rows where the behaviour itself is documented in an official source; every other row records a behaviour derived from the content demands of the skill and is tagged accordingly in `data/errors.json`.
