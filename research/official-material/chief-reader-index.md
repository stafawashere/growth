# Chief Reader Report Index

Chief Reader reports are written after each AP Reading by the Chief Reader for AP Calculus. Each report gives, for every free-response question, what the question asked, how responses performed, the common errors readers saw, and advice to teachers. The 2022 to 2024 reports were written by Julie Clark of Hollins University (cr-22:1, cr-23:1, cr-24:2); the 2025 report was written by Sharon Taylor of Georgia Southern University (crabbc-25:1).

Every report in the cache is a combined AB and BC document. Shared questions carry both AB and BC mean scores; AB-only and BC-only questions carry a single mean.

## Structure of each report [verified]

Each question section runs through the same four headings: what the responses were meant to demonstrate, how well the responses addressed the content and skills, what common misconceptions or knowledge gaps were seen, and what advice and resources the Chief Reader offers (cr-23:2 to cr-23:5). The misconceptions heading is presented as a two-column table pairing an observed gap with a response that demonstrates understanding (cr-24:3).

The 2025 report changes the score reporting format: it lists a maximum and a mean for each of the nine individual scoring points as well as an overall question mean, and it organises its commentary by scoring point rather than by narrative paragraph (crabbc-25:2 and crabbc-25:3). The 2022 to 2024 reports give only an overall mean per question (cr-22:2, cr-23:2, cr-24:2).

## Administrations not in the corpus [uncertain]

No Chief Reader report for the 2018, 2019, 2020 or 2021 administrations is present in the cache. Nothing in the four cached reports states whether such reports were published. The 2023 report does refer readers to the 2021 AB4/BC4 part (c) scoring guidelines and Chief Reader Report as a resource (cr-23:17), which is consistent with a 2021 report having existed, but that reference is not by itself confirmation, and no 2018, 2019 or 2020 report is mentioned anywhere in the cached text. The 2020 administration was shortened and delivered differently, so the existence and the shape of any report for that year are unknown. Whether reports for 2018 to 2021 were ever published is recorded here as unresolved.
