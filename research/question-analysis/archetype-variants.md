# AP Calculus BC Archetype Variants

394 variant records sit under the archetypes in [question-archetypes.md](question-archetypes.md). Each variant names one change to the surface of an archetype and records whether that change makes the question easier, harder, neutral, or dependent on the rest of the part. A variant may sit on more than one dimension, so the section counts below sum to more than 394.

## Which variations preserve the tested skill and which change it [inferred]

A variation preserves the tested skill when the invariant structure recorded on the archetype still describes the response. Changing the quantity being accumulated, the letters naming the variables, the units of a rate, or the number of rows in a table leaves the invariant untouched; those items are recorded as safe variables on each archetype and a variant built only from them is neutral.

A variation silently changes the tested skill when the invariant moves. Four patterns recur in the records. Supplying the derivative graph where the function graph was supplied turns a reading task into a relating task, as between BC-QA-05009 and BC-QA-05008. Removing the initial condition turns recovery of a value into a net change, as the variants under BC-QA-06005 show. Reversing the direction, so that an answer is supplied and a parameter is asked for, replaces execution of a procedure with an inverse solve, as under BC-QA-01008 and BC-QA-05002. Moving a part from the no-calculator side to the calculator side replaces the antidifferentiation work with a setup-plus-result obligation, as the calculator variants under BC-QA-06005 and BC-QA-08001 show.

Variants tagged harder or depends are the ones most often carrying a changed skill; variants tagged neutral are the ones built from safe variables. The difficulty factors those changes activate are catalogued in [difficulty-factors.md](difficulty-factors.md).
