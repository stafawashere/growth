## The setup-plus-result rule [verified]

The CED requires that a free response answer obtained from one of the four required capabilities be written with the setup that leads to it beside the result the calculator produced, and that any other calculator feature be accompanied by the mathematics it replaced. The rule and its sources are in [../exam/calculator-policy.md](../exam/calculator-policy.md). Its effect on the rubric is that a calculator part is commonly split into a setup point and an answer point, which is why the archetypes above with status calculator carry a scoring pattern naming both. BC-PT-99001, BC-PT-99005, and BC-PT-99018 are the point types that carry the setup obligation, and BC-PT-99004 is the answer point that a correct value earns with or without work.

The rule cuts both ways. A response that reports only a decimal earns the answer point and loses the setup point; a response that writes a correct setup and mistypes it earns the setup point and loses the answer point. Scoring conventions for that split are in [../scoring/scoring-patterns.md](../scoring/scoring-patterns.md) and [../scoring/partial-credit-patterns.md](../scoring/partial-credit-patterns.md).

## How one archetype changes between the two sides [inferred]

The 37 archetypes with status either are the ones whose invariant structure survives the move, and their variant records carry the change. Four patterns are visible in the records.

- BC-QA-06005 recovers a quantity from its rate and an initial condition. With a calculator the integrand need not antidifferentiate in closed form and the work is the setup plus the reported value; without one the integrand is chosen so that a substitution or a standard form closes it, and BC-DF-06 replaces BC-DF-07.
- BC-QA-08001 computes an average value. With a calculator the division and the integral are both written and a decimal is reported; without one the same formula is carried to an exact value and the arithmetic is the assessed step.
- BC-QA-04002 approximates a derivative from a table. The arithmetic is light enough that the calculator changes nothing about the reasoning, which is why the record sits at either and its variants split on the interval rather than on the device.
- BC-QA-02013 exists only because the device exists: the derivative at a point is produced numerically and the response must still name what was evaluated and where.

Archetypes with status no_calculator are the ones whose assessed step is the manipulation itself, such as the antidifferentiation techniques BC-QA-06008, BC-QA-06009, and BC-QA-06010, the rule manipulations in Units 2 and 3, and the series machinery of Unit 10, where a numerical answer would bypass the reasoning being scored.

## Precision and reporting [verified]

Decimal answers on free response questions must be accurate to three places after the decimal point, may be rounded or truncated to that accuracy, and an inappropriately rounded answer does not earn the point; within one free response question at most one point is lost to inappropriate rounding. Rounding is also a named practice skill, BC-MPS-4E. The sources are given in [../exam/calculator-policy.md](../exam/calculator-policy.md) and the scoring behaviour in [../scoring/scoring-patterns.md](../scoring/scoring-patterns.md) and [../scoring/notation-requirements.md](../scoring/notation-requirements.md). An intermediate value carried at three places into a later step is the usual route to a lost answer point, which is BC-DF-08 acting on a calculator part.
