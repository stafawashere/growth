# AP Calculus BC Historical Frequency of Question Types

This is the only file in the library permitted to hold frequency tables. Every table below is generated by `tools/frequency_tables.py` from `data/frq_records.json` and is reproduced here without alteration.

## What these counts are and are not [verified]

Every number in this file is an observed count over the administrations indexed in the corpus, and nothing more. The indexed years are 2012, 2013, 2014, 2015, 2018, 2019, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, and 2026, covering 249 question-part records.

- 2012 contributes one question only, Q6, so three part records. It is not a full administration and any row that names 2012 rests on that single question.
- 2018 is present as a free-response document with no scoring guideline in the corpus, so its 22 part records carry 0 points, no point types, and no scoring behaviours. Counts of parts include 2018; counts of points do not.
- 2021 is an anomalous administration. The cached document does not state which form its questions came from, so 2021 is excluded from every "years present" denominator. It is still listed in the year columns and still contributes part records, which is why a family can read "8 of 11" beside a year list holding nine years.
- 2016, 2017, and 2020 are absent from the corpus entirely. No count here says anything about those administrations.
- Questions from before 2019 were mapped onto the current CED framework by inference, because they predate it. Their unit, topic, skill, and archetype assignments are research conclusions, not labels the source documents carry.
- Recurrence is a property of the indexed corpus. A count of how often something has been observed is not a statement about any administration that has not been indexed, and nothing in this file should be read as one.

The denominators used throughout are 11 non-anomalous administrations, 12 indexed administrations, 67 questions, and 249 part records.
