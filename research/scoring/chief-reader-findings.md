# Chief Reader Findings, AP Calculus AB and BC, 2022 to 2025

Source documents are the cached Chief Reader reports `cr-22`, `cr-23`, `cr-24` and `crabbc-25`. Citations are given as document identifier and page, for example `cr-24:14`. Mean scores are reproduced as the reports state them, out of a maximum of 9 points per question. The 2025 report also publishes a mean for each of the nine individual scoring points, which earlier reports do not. No question stems or rubric text are reproduced.
