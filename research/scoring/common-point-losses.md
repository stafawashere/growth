# Common Point Losses in AP Calculus Free Response

Rows are grouped by the family of scoring point that is lost. A single mistake can cost points in more than one family, so a row names the family whose point the reports say was withheld. Every row cites the document and page where the report describes the loss. Counts are not given; the reports describe frequency in words, and those words are not converted into figures here.

## Setup points [verified]

A setup point asks for the equation, integral or expression that the work rests on, written before any value is reported. The reports treat an unsupported value as unsupported even when it is correct.

| Loss | Error records | Document and page |
|---|---|---|
| No calculator setup shown before a numerical answer | BC-ERR-99021 | cr-23:7, cr-24:4, cr-24:28, crabbc-25:30 |
| Differential omitted, leaving the integral expression ambiguous | BC-ERR-99006 | cr-23:28, cr-24:27, crabbc-25:23 |
| Riemann or trapezoidal sum shown as bare values rather than as products, or as the wrong sum type, or with a uniform width where the table gives unequal ones | BC-ERR-99028 | cr-23:4, cr-24:4, crabbc-25:13 |
| Integrand drawn from the wrong area or volume family, including a squared difference for an area and a missing constant on a revolution | BC-ERR-99011 | cr-23:31, cr-24:23, crabbc-25:7, crabbc-25:8 |
| Average value setup replaced by a difference quotient, or the derivative used as the integrand | BC-ERR-99015 | cr-23:4, crabbc-25:3 |
| Displacement setup given where total distance was asked | BC-ERR-99010 | cr-23:8, cr-24:8 |
| Parametric or vector quantity assembled from the wrong derivatives, including an inverted slope quotient and a separated distance integral | BC-ERR-99029 | cr-23:27, cr-24:27 |
| Polar area integrand written without squaring the radius, or with limits that do not match the intersection angles | BC-ERR-99031 | crabbc-25:29, crabbc-25:30 |
| Separable equation separated with a constant on the wrong side, or antidifferentiated incorrectly | BC-ERR-99014 | cr-23:12, cr-24:11, cr-24:12 |

## Answer points [verified]

An answer point is the reported value. It usually depends on the setup point, though several parts allow it to be earned independently.

| Loss | Error records | Document and page |
|---|---|---|
| Fewer than three digits after the decimal, or an intermediate value rounded before reuse | BC-ERR-99019 | cr-23:27, cr-24:10, cr-24:26, crabbc-25:8, crabbc-25:30 |
| Simplification that was never required introduces an arithmetic or algebra error | BC-ERR-99022 | cr-24:19, crabbc-25:26, crabbc-25:35 |
| Accumulation function evaluated without the value at the lower limit, or with the sign of reversed limits ignored | BC-ERR-99012 | cr-23:20, cr-24:14, crabbc-25:16 |
| Euler's method run with the wrong initial value, step size or slope | BC-ERR-99026 | cr-24:32, crabbc-25:34 |
| Integration by parts applied with the wrong sign, the wrong pieces, or misplaced bounds | BC-ERR-99025 | cr-23:32, cr-24:32 |
| General term of a differentiated series assembled or simplified incorrectly | BC-ERR-99018 | cr-23:35, cr-24:36, crabbc-25:38 |

## Units points [verified]

Units are scored separately from the value, and in several years the value was more often correct than the units accompanying it.

## Interpretation points [verified]

An interpretation point asks what a computed value means in the setting of the problem, in words, with the quantity and the interval named.

| Loss | Error records | Document and page |
|---|---|---|
| Interval omitted from an interpretation, for example an arc length named without the interval it covers | BC-ERR-99027 | cr-24:32 |
| A rate of a rate described as a rate, or the direction of its change omitted | BC-ERR-99027 | cr-23:4 |
| Key phrases absent from an average value interpretation | BC-ERR-99027 | cr-24:3 |
| A true statement given that does not answer the question asked | BC-ERR-99027, BC-ERR-99030 | cr-24:4 |

## Justification points [verified]

A justification point asks for mathematical reasons, and for the verification of any condition a theorem requires. It is the family the reports return to most often, and in 2025 the two points with the lowest means on their respective questions were both justification points (crabbc-25:15 and crabbc-25:28).

| Loss | Error records | Document and page |
|---|---|---|
| Hypotheses of the Mean Value Theorem, the Intermediate Value Theorem or L'Hospital's Rule not verified | BC-ERR-99008 | cr-23:4, cr-23:16, crabbc-25:12 |
| Local sign change offered where a global argument or a complete candidates test was required | BC-ERR-99004 | cr-23:16, crabbc-25:4, crabbc-25:18, crabbc-25:30 |
| Speed conclusion drawn from the sign of acceleration alone | BC-ERR-99003 | cr-23:7, cr-24:7, crabbc-25:22 |
| Reasoning not tied to the given graph, or tied to a derivative that was not supplied | BC-ERR-99023 | cr-23:16, crabbc-25:17 |
| Over or underestimate claimed without appealing to concavity | BC-ERR-99020 | cr-23:11, cr-23:12 |
| Conditions for an error bound not stated, or the error written as equal to the bound | BC-ERR-99016 | cr-23:35, cr-24:36, crabbc-25:34 |
| Endpoints of an interval of convergence untested, or tested with a comparison applied to an alternating series | BC-ERR-99017 | cr-24:35, crabbc-25:38 |

## Notation points [verified]

Notation losses rarely have a point of their own; they remove the reader's ability to credit work that is otherwise correct, and the reports say responses lacking notation are rarely eligible for all points (cr-24:28).

| Loss | Error records | Document and page |
|---|---|---|
| A variable expression equated to a numerical value, or successive lines joined by equals signs | BC-ERR-99002 | cr-23:19, cr-24:8, cr-24:27, crabbc-25:25 |
| Vague referent in a justification, such as it, the function, the graph or the slope | BC-ERR-99001 | cr-23:16, cr-24:11, crabbc-25:16, crabbc-25:18 |
| Differentials and derivatives mixed, or the variable of differentiation left unstated | BC-ERR-99013 | cr-23:24, cr-24:20, crabbc-25:26 |
| Limit notation introduced then dropped, or arithmetic written with the infinity symbol | BC-ERR-99007 | cr-23:16, cr-23:31 |
| Parentheses omitted when a given expression replaces a named function | BC-ERR-99009 | cr-23:30, cr-24:23, crabbc-25:7 |

## Precision and presentation points [verified]

This family covers losses where the mathematics is right and the presentation withholds the point.

| Loss | Error records | Document and page |
|---|---|---|
| Decimal presentation error carried through several parts of one response | BC-ERR-99019 | cr-24:26, crabbc-25:30 |
| Solution curve on a slope field that crosses an asymptote, stops short of the required extent, or shows a family rather than a particular solution | BC-ERR-99024 | cr-23:12, cr-24:11 |
| Integration by parts presented in a table without labels, so the technique is not evident | BC-ERR-99025 | cr-22:27, cr-23:32 |
| A correct answer boxed alongside a second, unrequested value, so the intended answer is the wrong one | BC-ERR-99022 | cr-24:3 |
